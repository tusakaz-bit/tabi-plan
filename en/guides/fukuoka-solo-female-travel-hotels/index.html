3 Tips for Choosing a Safe Hotel as a Solo Female Traveler

Fukuoka is generally a very safe city, but for complete peace of mind, we recommend hotels that offer these three features:

  • Station Proximity: Look for hotels within a 5-minute walk from major subway stations (like Hakata, Tenjin, or Nakasu-Kawabata) to minimize walking with luggage.
  • High Security: Accommodations with elevator keycard access and 24-hour front desk services ensure only registered guests can enter guest floors.
  • Ladies-Only Floors: Some premium budget hotels offer dedicated floors for women, featuring upgraded amenities and extra security.
