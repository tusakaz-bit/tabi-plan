Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency, Data Usage, and Partner Disclosures

Last Updated: November 2026

At TABI-PLAN (https://tabi-plan.org/), we respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how information is collected and how this website is funded.

1. Affiliate Disclosure

TABI-PLAN participates in various affiliate marketing programs, including Impact.com, Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Trip.com, Awin, and other travel partner networks.

This means that some of the links to hotels, travel services, and booking platforms on this website are affiliate links. If you click on an affiliate link and complete a reservation or purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. These commissions help us maintain the site and continue providing free travel guides.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require visitors to create an account or submit personal data to browse our guides. However, like most websites, we automatically collect certain non-personal technical data when you visit:

  • Browser type, device category, and operating system
  • Referring URLs, pages visited, and time spent on the site
  • General geographic location (country/city level) and IP address (anonymized where applicable)

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and third-party analytics tools, such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console, to understand how visitors interact with our content and to improve site performance.

  • Affiliate Tracking Cookies: When you click an outbound booking link, our affiliate partners may place a cookie on your browser solely to attribute the referral if you make a booking within their tracking window.
  • You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Websites

Our website contains links to external hotel booking platforms and travel service providers. Once you leave tabi-plan.org, your interactions and transactions are governed by the privacy policies and terms of service of those third-party websites. We do not collect or store any payment or reservation details.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your region (including the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data collected via cookies. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us using the details below.
