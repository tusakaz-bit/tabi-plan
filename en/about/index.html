About TABI-PLAN

Independent Japan Hotel & Travel Curation Guide

Welcome to TABI-PLAN (https://tabi-plan.org/), an independent travel and hotel curation guide dedicated to helping travelers discover comfortable, convenient, and well-located accommodations across Japan.

Our Mission

Planning a trip to Japan involves navigating thousands of hotel options, complex transit hubs, and varying local amenities. Our mission is to simplify accommodation research by curating reliable hotels based on practical criteria that matter most to travelers—such as station proximity, cleanliness, security, luggage convenience, and overall value.

What We Cover

  • Station-Front & Transit-Friendly Hotels: Carefully selected accommodations near major bullet train (Shinkansen) hubs and subway stations in cities like Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Fukuoka, and Sapporo.
  • Curated Area Guides: Neighborhood breakdowns designed for solo travelers, couples, families, and business visitors.
  • Practical Travel Insights: Clear highlights on room amenities, nearby dining and convenience stores, and access from major airports.

Editorial Independence

All hotel selections and travel guides on TABI-PLAN are researched and compiled from a local perspective in Japan. While we participate in affiliate marketing programs to support the ongoing operation of this website, our editorial recommendations are based on location utility, guest convenience, and verified facility features.
